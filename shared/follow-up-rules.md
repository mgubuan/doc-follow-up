# Follow-up rules

The rules live in `core/follow-up-rules.md`. Read that file. Do not follow a schedule written anywhere else.

Short version, so a quick read still points the right way:

- The initial email is touch 1.
- At most four emails, and the last one for a normal item is the first business day after the deadline.
- No email on Saturday or Sunday.
- Halfway rounds down (`floor`).
- After the planned emails are used up and the deadline has passed, status is Call needed. Do not keep emailing.
- A bounce is Bad contact. Stop email immediately.
