# Document Types

## Invoice Copies
- **Key details to request:** Invoice number, vendor/customer name, date or date range, amount if known
- **What to check on receipt:** Correct invoice number, matching date, all pages present, legible
- **Submission:** Reply to requesting email with PDF or photo attachment

## Receipt Copies
- **Key details to request:** Vendor name, approximate date, approximate amount, what was purchased
- **What to check on receipt:** Matching vendor, date within expected range, amount visible
- **Submission:** Reply to requesting email with photo or scan

## Bank Statements
- **Key details to request:** Bank name, account (last 4 digits if multiple), month/period needed
- **What to check on receipt:** Correct bank, correct account, correct month/period, all pages present, beginning and ending balances visible
- **Submission:** Reply with PDF (downloaded from online banking preferred)

## Forwarded Emails
- **Key details to request:** Who the email was from/to, approximate date, subject matter
- **What to check on receipt:** Relevant email thread, dates match, complete chain if needed
- **Submission:** Forward the original email to the requesting address

## Contracts
- **Key details to request:** Parties involved, type of contract, approximate date signed
- **What to check on receipt:** All pages present, signatures visible, correct parties, correct dates
- **Submission:** Reply with PDF or scanned copy

## Purchase Orders
- **Key details to request:** PO number, vendor name, date, items ordered
- **What to check on receipt:** Correct PO number, matching vendor, all line items visible
- **Submission:** Reply with PDF or copy

## Logs
- **Key details to request:** Type of log, date range, format (spreadsheet, handwritten, app export)
- **What to check on receipt:** Correct date range, all entries present, format is usable
- **Submission:** Reply with file attachment or photos
