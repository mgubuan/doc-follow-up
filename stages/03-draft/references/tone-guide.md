# Tone Guide

## The Rule

Every communication is friendly and professional. No exceptions. No escalation.

## Good Examples
- "Hope you are doing well!"
- "Just checking in on..."
- "We understand things get busy"
- "Thanks so much for getting this to us"

## Never Say
- "As previously stated..."
- "Per our last email..."
- "This is our fourth request..."
- "We require immediate attention to..."
- Any language implying blame, frustration, or consequences beyond factual deadline impact

## The Test

Before sending: would you be comfortable receiving this email from YOUR accountant? If it would make you feel nagged, guilty, or defensive, rewrite it.
